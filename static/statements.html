cal-agent

Cal card charges, by category

Load your Cal (כאל) statement PDFs here, and the agent will read and analyze every charge on demand from a caller agent.

  1. Sign in with Google: you see and delete only your own files.
  2. Load your statement PDFs: under any file name; each is filed under its billing month.
  3. Charges are categorized on request: when a caller agent asks for a month, the agent reads it, and AI picks a category only for a merchant it hasn't categorized for you before.
  • Every amount and date is read from the statement itself; AI only picks each merchant's category.
  • A month comes back whole or not at all: its charges are checked against the total the statement prints.
  • Installments, standing orders and purchases abroad are read the way Cal prints them.
  • When a month's statement is missing, the app that asked for it waits; once you load it here, the review goes on.
  • cal-agent never logs in to the Cal site: it reads only the statements you load.
  • One statement per month: to replace one, delete the old file first.

Signing in with Google is safer and easier than opening an account with a new password.

  • Google approves the sign-in. Google gives the app only a confirmation of who you are, with no access to your data (emails, contacts and more).
  • You stay in control: you can disconnect the app at any time in your Google account's settings.
  • No new password to remember, and none that could leak from here: the app stores no passwords at all.
  • Google's protections work for you: two-step verification and alerts on a suspicious sign-in guard your sign-in here too.
